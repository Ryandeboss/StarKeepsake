# StarKeepsake showroom visual assets
The showroom currently uses **photographic** interior backgrounds and real wood-grain texture photographs.
They are visual references only. They are not photos of a manufactured StarKeepsake, not a user's room, and not exact augmented reality.

- Warm walnut sample: Rachel Kelli, "A close-up of dark brown wood grain", https://unsplash.com/photos/dark-brown-wood-grain-texture-n7MMvJFAp8Q
  Image source: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1621295693450-080546d2ec8e
- Light oak / maple sample: Ted Balmer, "A close up of a wood grain texture" (maple), https://unsplash.com/photos/a-close-up-of-a-wood-grain-texture-rq__zHQtSv0

The photographic texture is color-graded in CSS to suggest wood finish choices; it does not prove the final supplier's grain or varnish.
